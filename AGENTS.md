# Website editing preferences

## Selected publications

The owner explicitly prefers a graphical abstract on the left and publication details on the right. The paper title must link directly to its DOI/journal online version. Retain authors and publication venue as secondary metadata, followed by 2–3 short owner-provided highlights. Italicize scientific species names.

Do not add Project, Code, Citation counts, BibTeX, or other publication action buttons.

For every future selected publication, ask the owner for its graphical abstract/image and the short paragraphs or highlights introducing the work if they have not already supplied them. Do not invent replacement images or write the highlights without asking. Reuse material already provided instead of asking again.

Keep publication content in `profile.js`, under `publications`; use `image`, `imageAlt`, `paper`, and `highlights`. A missing image must never be replaced with a fabricated graphical abstract.

Publish website changes to this GitHub Pages repository, as requested by the owner. Preserve unrelated files and changes.
